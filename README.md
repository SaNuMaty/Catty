Catty is a small caterpillar with a huge apetite for apples. Will you help her eat as many as she can? Or will she perish to the looming threat of the birds?

This game is on version 0.1 and it's still in very rough shape. I will continue to update it and make it even more refined!

The game is free, but all donations will go towards helping me out and giving me the funds to pay the fees to publish the game for example on steam!


Movement
up_arrow - up;
down_arrow - down;
left_arrow - left;
right_arrow - right;

You can move on the diagonals as well by pressing 2 keys at the same time!

Installation Tutorial:

Grab the .exe and follow the installation shown on screen.

WARNING!!!111!!11!

When installing Windows may show a smart screen protection screen. If you are uncomfortable with this, I am really sorry :C I am working on getting it signed or published in a game store. You may check yourself on software like virus total that it does not flag it as a virus. If you decide to procede just press execute anyways on the drop down menu after the warning.

Have fun! And thank you for downloading!
